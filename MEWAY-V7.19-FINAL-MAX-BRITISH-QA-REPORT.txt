MEWAY V7.19 — FINAL MAX BRITISH CURRICULUM — QA REPORT

Scope
- Built on V7.18 without redesigning the MEWAY interface architecture.
- British English remains the primary language policy.
- Added 60 further lexical themes / 480 curated candidate entries before global de-duplication.
- Added per-theme quiz, two games, two challenges and two missions.
- Added a dictionary search field that filters the already loaded client-side data. Typing does not create a Cloudflare request.
- Existing level/category filters remain intact.

Quality controls
- New lexical candidates are globally de-duplicated against existing word content before insertion.
- Empty English keys are rejected.
- New quiz/mission options are generated from distinct entries in the same curated theme.
- Existing user-requested emergency/accident-topic runtime exclusion remains applied after this expansion.
- No third-party lesson text is copied into this expansion.
- CEFR A1–C2 is used as the proficiency organisation model; British English is the language variety policy.

Important release note
No automated system can truthfully certify that a large educational corpus contains literally zero linguistic errors. This build is a structured QA release, not an official certification by the Council of Europe, British Council, Cambridge, or another third party. Independent human editorial review remains appropriate before a commercial launch.
