MEWAY V7.5 — MAX CLOUD EFFICIENCY

- Existing D1 tables/data are not deleted or reset.
- Progress answers and ordinary UI activity are accumulated locally and flushed as one PUT after 20 seconds of inactivity.
- Pending progress is also flushed when the Activity is hidden/closed (pagehide/visibilitychange).
- XP-changing completions and Daily Mission rewards flush immediately so Discord level roles are not delayed.
- Public content remains session-cached on the client and 10-minute cached in the Worker isolate.
- Leaderboard modes are sorted client-side after one load.
- Smart Coach, Flight Path, level-up animation, filters and UI interactions do not create progress writes.
- Progress GET verifies the real Discord member role every load. D1 write on GET is suppressed unless useful (new user/day, repaired role, identity change, or 15-minute last-seen checkpoint).

- Real event history is stored inside the existing progress JSON (level-up, streak milestones, mission/material completion) and displayed in Admin > Students event center.
