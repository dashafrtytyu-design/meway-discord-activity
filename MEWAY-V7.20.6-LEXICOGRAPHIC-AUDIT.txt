MEWAY V7.20.6 — LEXICOGRAPHIC AUDIT

Scope
- Existing Word Vault entries were normalised and audited.
- Global English-entry duplicate guard added across A1–C2.
- Duplicate senses should be represented inside one card rather than as duplicate cards.
- Existing V7.20.4 prohibited-topic filter remains active.
- British English remains the house standard.
- Existing local transparent SVG visual system remains active; no emoji fallback is used for dictionary art.

Reference framework used for verification methodology
- Oxford Learner's Dictionaries: Oxford 3000 / Oxford 5000, CEFR-aligned core vocabulary.
- English Vocabulary Profile: CEFR A1–C2 words, meanings, phrases, idioms and collocations.
- Cambridge Dictionary / Essential British English: British-English sense and pronunciation cross-check reference.
- FreeDict English–Russian: open bilingual cross-check source.

IMPORTANT QUALITY LIMIT
This build does NOT claim that all 5,000 desired cards have been individually lexicographically certified. Bulk-copying proprietary dictionary entries is not used. The archive contains the actually audited current corpus and a hard global no-duplicate guard. Any future expansion must pass the same checks before being included.

Counts after global deduplication are recorded in MEWAY-V7.20.6-COUNTS.txt.
