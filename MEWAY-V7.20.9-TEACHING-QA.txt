MEWAY V7.20.9 — TEACHING QA HARDENING

Changes are limited to the dictionary data layer.
- Global duplicate guard retained and strengthened: duplicate headwords are merged into the first card as additional senses rather than silently discarded.
- Empty/malformed dictionary records are removed locally.
- English/Russian whitespace and IPA delimiters are normalised.
- Misleading internal wording that called generated material “verified” was removed.
- No new Worker/D1 request is introduced by these checks.
- Existing XP, missions, games, admin architecture and Cloud Saver logic are otherwise unchanged.

Publication note:
This package is QA-hardened, but no claim is made that every IPA/translation/example has been independently certified by a third-party dictionary. Third-party dictionary content is not copied wholesale into MEWAY.
